How to get help

1Open the Lab Helpdesk

Click the Lab Helpdesk button at the bottom right and choose Student or Teacher. No sign-in is needed.

2Describe the problem

Write in English or Taglish and include the unit number, for example PC-12.

3Follow the steps or file a request

If the steps do not fix it, the helpdesk files a ticket for the Lab Administrator.

Emergency: if you see sparks or smoke or smell something burning, step away from the unit and tell your teacher at once.
