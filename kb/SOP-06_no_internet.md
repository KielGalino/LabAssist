# SOP-06: No Internet / Blocked Websites

**Applies to:** Internet access on lab thin clients (MikroTik router, TP-Link Omada switch).
**Common phrases:** no internet, can't open website, page won't load, site blocked, access denied, walang internet, ayaw mag-load, walang connection, naka-block ang site, hindi mabuksan ang website

## Check your unit
1. Open another website (e.g. the school website) to see if only one site fails.
2. Close and reopen the browser.
3. Ask your teacher to restart your session from MultiPoint Dashboard (SOP-04).
4. If other units near you also have no internet, inform the teacher.

## Blocked sites (web filter)
1. The lab uses a web filter on the MikroTik router. Social media, games, streaming and adult sites are blocked during class.
2. A "blocked" or "access denied" page means the filter is working - it is not a fault.
3. Do not try to get around the filter (VPNs, proxies); this violates the lab rules (SOP-13).
4. Teachers who need a site unblocked for a lesson must request it by ticket at least 1 school day before.

## Whole lab has no internet
1. The teacher checks if the school internet is down (ask the ICT office).
2. Do not touch the router, switch or cables in the network cabinet.

## Escalate when
1. One unit has no internet while others work - ticket, medium priority.
2. The whole lab is offline - **critical** ticket, category Network.
3. A needed educational site is blocked - teacher submits a ticket (category Network).
