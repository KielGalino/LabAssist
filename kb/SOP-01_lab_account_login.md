# SOP-01: Thin Client Login Problems

**Applies to:** Students and teachers when a thin client (PC-01 to PC-42) shows the login screen, says the station is locked, or will not log in.
**Common phrases:** can't log in, cannot login, PC is locked, station locked, account locked, asking for password, login screen, invalid password, naka-lock ang PC, naka-lock yung computer, ayaw mag-login, hindi makapasok, humihingi ng password, nag-logout ang PC

## How lab login works
1. There are no individual student accounts in the lab.
2. Each thin client (PC-01 to PC-42) has its own station account. Its password is known only to teachers and the Lab Administrator.
3. At the start of class, the teacher logs in each station. Students are never given a station password.

## Station shows the login screen
1. Do not type any password and do not try to guess it.
2. Raise your hand and ask your teacher to log the station in.
3. While waiting, check that the keyboard is working (Caps Lock and Num Lock lights respond) so the teacher can log in quickly (SOP-10).

## Station says it is locked
1. A station locks automatically after **5 failed login attempts** and stays locked for **15 minutes**.
2. Do not try again during the 15 minutes - every new attempt keeps the station locked.
3. Tell your teacher and, if the teacher allows, move to a free unit.
4. After 15 minutes, ask the teacher to log the station in again.

## Login screen does not appear
1. If the screen is black, follow SOP-03 (black screen / no signal).
2. If the screen says "Disconnected" or "Cannot reach server", follow SOP-05.

## Escalate when
1. The station is still locked after 15 minutes and the teacher cannot log it in - give LabAssist the unit number and it will file an **unlock request** (see SOP-02).
2. You see someone else's files or session on the station, or suspect someone tried to break in.
