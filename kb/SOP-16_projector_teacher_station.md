# SOP-16: Projector and Teacher Station

**Applies to:** The classroom projector and the teacher station running MultiPoint Dashboard.
**Common phrases:** projector no display, projector not working, screen not showing on projector, MultiPoint Dashboard, lock student screens, ayaw mag-display ng projector, walang lumalabas sa projector, paano i-project, paano i-lock ang screens

## Projector shows no display
1. Turn on the projector with the remote and wait 30 seconds for the lamp to warm up.
2. Set the projector input to **HDMI 1** (press Source/Input on the remote).
3. Check that the HDMI cable is firmly connected to the teacher station.
4. On the teacher station press `Windows + P` and choose **Duplicate**.
5. If the image is blurry, adjust the focus ring on the projector lens.

## Teacher station and MultiPoint Dashboard
1. Log in to the teacher station with your teacher ID.
2. Open **MultiPoint Dashboard** to see all student stations.
3. Use it to lock or unlock student screens, project your screen to students, send messages, and log off a frozen station.
4. If a student station is missing in the Dashboard, the unit may be off or disconnected (SOP-05).

## Escalate when
1. Projector lamp is dead, shows a warning light, or there is no image on HDMI 1 - ticket, category Hardware, **high** priority if during class.
2. MultiPoint Dashboard does not open - ticket, category Server.
