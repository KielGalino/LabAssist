# SOP-03: Thin Client Black Screen / No Signal

**Applies to:** Thin client units PC-01 to PC-42 and their monitors.
**Common phrases:** black screen, no signal, blank screen, monitor not turning on, screen is off, itim ang screen, walang display, walang signal, patay ang monitor, ayaw bumukas ng monitor

## Quick checks
1. Check that the monitor power LED is on. If not, press the monitor power button.
2. Move the mouse or press a key - the unit may be in sleep mode.
3. Check that the monitor cable (HDMI/VGA) is firmly connected to the thin client.
4. Check that the thin client power LED is on. If off, press its power button once.

## Restart the thin client
1. Hold the thin client power button for 5 seconds to turn it off.
2. Wait 10 seconds, then press the power button again.
3. Wait up to 1 minute for the MultiPoint login screen.
4. If the monitor says "No signal", select the correct input (HDMI or VGA) using the monitor's menu button.

## If the whole row is black
1. Several black screens in the same area usually mean a power strip (AVR) is off - inform the teacher.
2. Do not unplug or move power cables yourself.

## Escalate when
1. The unit still has no display after a restart - create a ticket with the unit number (medium priority).
2. Several units are black at the same time (high priority).
3. You smell burning or see sparks - step away and report as **critical** (see SOP-14).
