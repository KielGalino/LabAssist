# SOP-14: Reporting Damaged Equipment

**Applies to:** All lab equipment: thin clients, monitors, keyboards, mice, headsets, servers, printer, projector, network devices, power strips.
**Common phrases:** broken, damaged, sparks, smoke, burning smell, cracked screen, report damage, sira, nasira, may usok, umuusok, may spark, basag ang monitor, nasunog, may amoy sunog

## Safety first (sparks, smoke, burning smell)
1. Step away from the equipment immediately. Do NOT touch it or unplug it.
2. Tell the teacher right away; the teacher switches off the power at the breaker if safe.
3. Report it as a **critical** ticket at once with the unit number and location.
4. Do not continue using nearby units until the lab staff says it is safe.

## Priority table
| Priority | When |
|----------|------|
| **Critical** | Sparks, smoke, burning smell, server down, whole lab offline |
| **High** | Several units down, or printer/projector not working during class |
| **Medium** | One unit (thin client, monitor, keyboard, mouse) not working or damaged |
| **Low** | Cosmetic damage (scratches, missing labels, loose keycap) |

## How to report
1. Give the unit number (PC-01 to PC-42, MPS-1/2/3, LAB-PRINTER-01, projector).
2. Describe what is damaged and what happened.
3. LabAssist creates a ticket (TKT-####) and alerts the administrator for high and critical tickets.
4. Put a "Do not use" note on the unit if the teacher asks.

## Escalate when
1. Any critical issue - it is escalated automatically.
2. A ticket is not acted on within 2 school days - check its status and ask for escalation.
