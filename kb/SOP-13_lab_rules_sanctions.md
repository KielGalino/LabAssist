# SOP-13: Lab Rules and Sanctions

**Applies to:** Everyone using the CSIS ICT Laboratory.
**Common phrases:** lab rules, can I eat in the lab, bawal, allowed, penalty, sanction, can I play games, bawal ba kumain, pwede ba mag-cellphone, anong parusa, rules sa lab

## General rules
1. No food or drinks inside the lab.
2. Enter only with a teacher or during your scheduled class (see SOP-15).
3. Do not attempt to log in to stations or guess station passwords; only teachers log stations in (SOP-01).
4. No games, social media or streaming unless the teacher allows it for the lesson.
5. Do not install, download or run unauthorized programs (SOP-11).
6. Do not unplug, move or open any equipment, cables or network devices.
7. Do not try to get around the web filter, security settings or monitoring.
8. Report damage immediately (SOP-14); close your programs and push your chair in before leaving.

## Sanctions
1. First offense: verbal warning and note in the lab logbook.
2. Second offense: written report to the adviser and 1-week suspension of lab privileges.
3. Damage caused by misuse: the student repairs or replaces the item, plus guidance office referral.
4. Hacking attempts, password guessing or filter bypass: lab access restricted and referral to the discipline office.

## Escalate when
1. A rule violation causes damage or a security incident - teacher reports via ticket or escalation.
