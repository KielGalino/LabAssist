# SOP-10: Keyboard and Mouse Problems

**Applies to:** USB keyboards and mice connected to the thin clients.
**Common phrases:** mouse not working, keyboard not working, keys not typing, cursor not moving, mouse lagging, ayaw gumana ng mouse, ayaw mag-type ng keyboard, hindi gumagalaw ang cursor, sira ang mouse, sira ang keyboard

## Quick checks
1. Check that the cable is firmly plugged into the thin client's USB port.
2. Unplug it, wait 5 seconds, and plug it into another USB port.
3. Check Num Lock (for number keys) and Caps Lock.
4. For an optical mouse, use it on a flat, non-shiny surface and clean the sensor.

## If it still does not work
1. Ask your teacher to restart your session from MultiPoint Dashboard (SOP-04).
2. Restart the thin client (hold power 5 seconds).
3. Do NOT swap keyboards or mice with other units yourself - tell the teacher.

## Escalate when
1. The device is physically broken (missing keys, broken cable, cracked mouse) - ticket, category Hardware, medium priority, include the unit number.
2. Several units have dead keyboards/mice at the same time - high priority (may be a server issue, see SOP-05).
