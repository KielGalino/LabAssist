# SOP-15: Lab Schedule and Reservations

**Applies to:** Lab opening hours and room reservations.
**Common phrases:** lab hours, lab schedule, is the lab open, reserve the lab, book the lab, maintenance day, anong oras bukas ang lab, bukas ba ang lab, pwede ba magreserve, sarado ba ang lab sa Sabado

## Lab hours
1. Open **Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM**.
2. Closed on weekends and holidays.
3. **Maintenance every first Saturday of the month** - no classes; servers may be restarted and updated.
4. Students may use the lab only during their scheduled class or with a teacher present.

## Reserving the lab (teachers)
1. Create a ticket with category **Lab Reservation**: date, time, section, number of units and purpose.
2. Reserve at least **2 school days** in advance.
3. Regular class schedules have priority over reservations.
4. You will get a confirmation through the ticket status.

## Escalate when
1. Two classes are scheduled in the lab at the same time - ticket to the lab administrator.
2. An urgent reservation (e.g. make-up exam) is needed in less than 2 days - high priority ticket.
