# SOP-07: Teacher Wi-Fi (CSIS-Faculty)

**Applies to:** Teachers and staff using the lab Wi-Fi. There is **no student Wi-Fi** in the lab.
**Common phrases:** wifi not working, can't connect to wifi, wifi login page, captive portal, faculty wifi, walang wifi, ayaw kumonek sa wifi, paano mag-connect sa wifi, student wifi

## How to connect
1. Select the Wi-Fi network **CSIS-Faculty** (TP-Link Omada access point).
2. A login page (captive portal) opens automatically. If not, open any website.
3. Log in with your **employee ID** and your portal password.
4. Each teacher may connect at most **2 devices**.

## Common problems
1. Login page does not appear: turn Wi-Fi off and on, then open `http://neverssl.com`.
2. "Device limit reached": disconnect one of your other devices first (max 2 devices).
3. Connected but no internet: forget the network, reconnect and log in again.
4. Students asking for Wi-Fi: there is no student Wi-Fi; students use the thin clients.

## Escalate when
1. Your employee ID is not accepted on the portal - ticket, category Network.
2. CSIS-Faculty does not appear at all - ticket, high priority if during class.
3. LabAssist never gives out Wi-Fi or router passwords.
