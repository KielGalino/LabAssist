# SOP-12: Audio and Headset Problems

**Applies to:** Headsets and speakers used with the thin clients.
**Common phrases:** no sound, can't hear, headset not working, microphone not working, audio problem, walang tunog, ayaw tumunog, hindi marinig, sira ang headset, walang sound

## Quick checks
1. Plug the headset into the thin client's headphone jack or USB port (not the monitor).
2. Click the speaker icon on the taskbar and make sure it is not muted; raise the volume.
3. Right-click the speaker icon > Sound settings and choose the correct output device.
4. Check the volume control or mute switch on the headset cable.

## Microphone
1. In Sound settings > Input, choose the headset microphone.
2. Allow microphone access in the app (browser pop-up).

## If it still does not work
1. Unplug and re-plug the headset, then ask your teacher to restart your session from MultiPoint Dashboard.
2. Try the headset on another unit (with the teacher's permission) to see if the headset is broken.

## Escalate when
1. The headset is broken (no sound on any unit, cracked, cut cable) - ticket, category Hardware, low or medium priority.
2. No unit on the same server has sound - high priority (see SOP-05).
