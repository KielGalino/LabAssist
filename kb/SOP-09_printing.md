# SOP-09: Printing

**Applies to:** The lab printer LAB-PRINTER-01.
**Common phrases:** can't print, printer not working, print stuck, paper jam, printer offline, no ink, ayaw mag-print, hindi makapag-print, na-jam ang papel, walang lumalabas sa printer, paano mag-print

## Before printing
1. Students may print only with the teacher's permission.
2. Select the printer **LAB-PRINTER-01** in the print window.
3. Use Print Preview to avoid wasting paper.

## Print job does not come out
1. Check that LAB-PRINTER-01 is selected (not "Microsoft Print to PDF").
2. Open Settings > Printers & scanners > LAB-PRINTER-01 > Open queue; wait if other jobs are ahead.
3. Do not send the same document many times - this jams the queue.
4. Ask the teacher to check the printer display for errors (offline, no paper, low toner).

## Paper jam
1. Only the teacher or lab staff may open the printer covers.
2. Turn the printer off before removing jammed paper; pull the paper out slowly in the direction of the paper path.
3. Never use metal objects to remove paper.

## Escalate when
1. Printer shows an error after a restart, or toner is empty - ticket, category Printing.
2. Printer is down during a class that needs printing - **high** priority.
